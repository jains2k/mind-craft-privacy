Privacy Policy

Una (formerly Mind Craft) — Last updated September 26, 2026

Information We Collect

Una collects limited, pseudonymous information to provide app functionality and understand how the app is used. When analytics is enabled, Firebase Analytics may collect:

  • Device ID (IDFV), used for app functionality and analytics
  • Product interactions, such as sessions, block starts, completions, and feature use
  • Other usage data, including session or block durations, completion metrics, and general device and operating-system information
  • App analytics properties such as install ID, app version, locale, user type, and voice preference

When you voluntarily submit feedback, Una sends your optional rating or NPS response, a free-text comment (up to 1,000 characters), and basic app/device metadata such as install ID, app version, build number, iOS version, locale, and submission time to Firebase Firestore.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to provide app functionality, understand usage, troubleshoot issues, and improve Una. We do not use it for advertising, cross-app tracking, or profiling, and we do not sell personal information.

What We Do Not Collect

Una does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, HealthKit data, or health details. We do not send raw AI prompts, generated routine titles or content, or other personal meditation details to our analytics systems.

Data Sharing

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Firestore are operated by Google and process data on our behalf. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to data brokers or advertisers. The data described above is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking.

See Google's privacy and Firebase information for more details about Google's processing.

Data Retention

Analytics data is retained by Firebase for up to 14 months by default. You can request deletion of data associated with your installation by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Una is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of analytics or feedback data associated with your installation by contacting us at the email below.

Health Data

Una does not access or store health data from your device. The app does not use HealthKit.
